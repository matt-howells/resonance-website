SHIFT

Resonance Collective's new hybrid series of events - SHIFT - attempts to provide deeper context to the dancefloor and offer alternative modes of engagement with the club, nightlife and dance music culture. Each dance music event will be paired with a workshop or artist talk that gets to the crux of a force crucial to the success of the former, featuring an arrangement of DJs, VJs, community organizers, producers, and theorists from areas both local and global. We are focusing on innovation, genre experimentation, scene-building, past, present, futures, and ways to think about what exactly it is we are doing here.
