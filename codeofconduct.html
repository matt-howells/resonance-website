  • ⍝ code of conduct

PLAY IT SAFE

Hydrate, pace yourself, and know your limits. Ear plugs are a good move. If you're using substances, get them tested. Take care of yourself and your friends. Look out for others too - if someone seems off or overwhelmed, check in on them.

CONSENT IS A MUST

Respect personal space and boundaries. Before getting close to, dancing with, touching, buying a drink for, or snapping a photo of someone - always ask first. If someone says no or gives off that energy, respect it and back off. No pressure, no problem.

NO HATE, NO HARM

Zero tolerance for discrimiation or aggression towards others - tat includes transphobia, racism, ableism, sexism, homophobia, and any kind of bigotry. If you're harassing, crossing boundaries, bullying, or maksing others feel unsafe, expect to be called on it and removed.

COLLABORATIVE COMMUNITY CARE

We're here to engage with the music and have a good time, so come with care and respect. Each of us is a vital part of this community- dancers, artists and organizers alike. This means we all contribute to making the sapce safe and welcoming. If you see someone struggling, feeling unsafe, or things just don't feel right, connect with a crew member. We're here to help - you'll find us with green Resonance lanyards floating around.
